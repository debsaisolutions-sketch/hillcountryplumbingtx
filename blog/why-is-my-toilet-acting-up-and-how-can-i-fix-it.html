Why Is My Toilet Acting Up and How Can I Fix It?

Dealing with a malfunctioning toilet can be both frustrating and inconvenient. Fortunately, many common toilet issues can be resolved with a little DIY knowledge. In this guide, we'll explore frequent toilet problems and provide solutions, while also highlighting when it might be time to call in the professionals at txkerrvilleplumbing.com.

Common Toilet Problems and DIY Solutions

1. Toilet Won't Stop Running

A constantly running toilet is not only annoying but can also lead to higher water bills. This issue is often caused by a faulty flapper or fill valve. Start by checking the flapper for signs of wear and tear. If it's damaged, replace it with a new one. If the issue persists, inspect the fill valve and consider adjusting or replacing it.

2. Weak Flush

If your toilet isn't flushing with enough power, there may be a clog or an issue with the flush valve. Begin by using a plunger to clear any potential blockages in the drain. For more persistent issues, the flush valve might need an adjustment or replacement.

3. Toilet Leaks at the Base

A leak at the base of the toilet is often due to a worn-out wax ring. To fix this, you'll need to remove the toilet, replace the wax ring, and then reseat the toilet securely. This can be a challenging task, so if you're not comfortable doing it yourself, it's best to contact a local plumber.

When to Call a Professional

While many toilet issues can be tackled with basic plumbing skills, some problems require professional assistance. Consider calling a Kerrville plumbing expert if:

  • You've tried DIY solutions, but the problem persists.
  • You're facing recurrent clogs or sewage backups.
  • You find water leaks that you cannot locate or access easily.

Keep Your Toilet in Top Shape

Regular maintenance can prevent many toilet problems from occurring in the first place. Simple steps like cleaning your toilet regularly, inspecting parts periodically, and being mindful of what you flush can make a big difference.
