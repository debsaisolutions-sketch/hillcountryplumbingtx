How Can You Fix a Leaking Toilet or Tub in the Hill Country?

Leaking toilets and tubs are common plumbing issues that can cause significant water waste and damage if left unchecked. Whether you're dealing with persistent drips or more serious leaks, it's essential to address these problems promptly. Here’s a guide to help you fix these leaks in your Hill Country home.

Identifying the Source of the Leak

The first step in fixing a leak is determining its source. For toilets, leaks often occur at the base or tank. Tubs may leak from the faucet, drain, or even the tub body itself.

Common Toilet Leak Sources

  • Base Leaks: Check the wax ring seal for wear. If water pools around the base, the seal may need replacing.
  • Tank Leaks: Inspect the flapper, fill valve, and tank bolts for signs of wear or damage.

Common Tub Leak Sources

  • Faucet Leaks: Dripping faucets often result from worn washers or cartridges.
  • Drain Leaks: Ensure the drain assembly is tightly sealed and free from corrosion.

DIY Fixes for Leaking Toilets and Tubs

Once you've identified the leak source, you can often perform simple repairs yourself. Here are some DIY solutions:

Fixing a Leaking Toilet

  • Replace the Wax Ring: Turn off the water supply, drain the tank, and lift the toilet to replace the wax ring. This can help stop leaks at the base.
  • Repair the Flapper: A worn flapper can cause continuous leaks into the bowl. Replace it to resolve the issue.
  • Tighten Tank Bolts: If water seeps from the tank, ensure the bolts are secure, but be careful not to over-tighten and crack the porcelain.

Fixing a Leaking Tub

  • Replace Faucet Washers: Shut off water to the tub and replace worn washers or cartridges in the faucet.
  • Seal the Drain: Use plumber’s putty or a new rubber gasket to ensure the drain is properly sealed.

When to Call a Kerrville Plumbing Expert
