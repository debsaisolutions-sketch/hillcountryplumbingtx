What Can I Do About a Leaky Faucet or Showerhead Before It Gets Worse?

Dealing with a leaky faucet or showerhead can be a frustrating experience for any homeowner in the Texas Hill Country. These minor annoyances can lead to increased water bills and potential water damage if not addressed promptly. Here's a guide to help you tackle these leaks and understand when it's time to call in a Kerrville plumbing professional.

Identifying the Source of the Leak

Before you can fix a leak, it's essential to determine its source. In most cases, faucets and showerheads leak due to worn-out washers, gaskets, or seals. Here’s how you can identify the problem:

  • Faucet Leak: Check for water dripping from the spout or pooling around the base.
  • Showerhead Leak: Look for drips from the head even when the shower is turned off or water seeping at the connection where the showerhead attaches to the pipe.

DIY Steps to Fix a Leaky Faucet

If you find your faucet is leaking, you can try these steps:

  1. Turn Off the Water Supply: Locate the shut-off valves under the sink and turn them off.
  2. Disassemble the Faucet: Use a screwdriver to remove the handle and access the internal components.
  3. Inspect and Replace Parts: Check washers, gaskets, and O-rings for wear and replace them as needed.
  4. Reassemble and Test: Put the faucet back together and turn the water supply back on to check for leaks.

Fixing a Leaky Showerhead

For a leaky showerhead, follow these tips:

  • Check the Connection: Ensure the showerhead is screwed on tightly. Use plumber’s tape for a better seal if needed.
  • Clean the Showerhead: Mineral deposits can cause leaks. Soak the showerhead in vinegar to remove buildup.
  • Replace Worn Seals: If the leak persists, inspect the rubber washer inside the showerhead and replace it if necessary.

When to Call a Kerrville Plumbing Professional

If your DIY efforts do not resolve the issue, or if you encounter more complex problems such as corroded pipes or persistent leaks, it’s time to contact a local Kerrville plumbing expert. Professional plumbers in the Texas Hill Country are equipped to handle more extensive repairs and ensure your plumbing system functions efficiently.
